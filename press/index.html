New Book Reveals Why Government ERP Projects Fail at Catastrophic Rates — and Delivers the Framework to Prevent It

For Release: Spring 2026

COLUMBIA, MD — Abhijit Verekar, five-time Inc. 5000 CEO and founder of Avèro Advisors, announces the publication of Start at Zero: Leading High-Stakes Modernization in the Age of AI, the first book to address the structural causes behind billion-dollar government technology failures.

Government ERP projects fail at rates that would be scandalous in any other sector. Over 70% of large government IT projects fail to deliver intended benefits, with notable disasters like Birmingham City Council's $216 million overrun on a $19 million budget. Verekar's book reveals that these failures aren't caused by bad technology — they're caused by conflicted advisory structures, absent governance, and skipped preparation.

"Every government leader facing a modernization decision right now is walking into a market that's more dangerous and more confusing than any time in the last twenty years," says Verekar. "The convergence of SAP end-of-support deadlines, Oracle cloud mandates, and AI hype has created conditions where billions of public dollars are at risk."

Start at Zero introduces the Phase Zero framework — a structured 100-day preparation methodology that creates clarity before vendors enter the conversation. The book includes 16 ready-to-use templates covering everything from governance charters to vendor evaluation scorecards to AI readiness assessments.

Drawing from over 30 government ERP engagements and two decades of working exclusively on the government's side of the table, the book provides the first comprehensive playbook for independent modernization oversight.
